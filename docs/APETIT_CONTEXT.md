# Apetit: real-world context for the pitch

Research from Apetit's public reports and announcements (checked 6 Nov 2026). Use it to tie our solution to problems Apetit has actually had.
**Rule:** the *problems* below are real; every *number our system produces* comes from the synthetic twin. Say "in the twin" or "in the synthetic data" every time.

## Company snapshot (Annual Report 2025)

- **Business:** a Finnish plant-based food company with two segments.
  - **Food Solutions:** frozen vegetables and ready meals, made in Säkylä, Pudasjärvi and Bjuv (Sweden).
  - **Oilseed Products:** the Kantvik vegetable-oil mill.
- **Size:** net sales **€167.6M** in 2025 (2024: €162.6M); **321** employees.
- **Strategy 2026–28, "A Season of Growth":** growth from **frozen peas**, **the Swedish market**, BlackGrain rapeseed protein, and more vegetable-oil capacity. Target operating profit above €10M by 2028.
- **Recent changes:**
  - Acquired Foodhills AB (Swedish frozen peas) on 27 Nov 2025; it was renamed Apetit Sverige AB in Feb 2026.
  - Decided to close the Pudasjärvi pizza factory (Apr 2026).
  - Agreed to sell the Kantvik biosteam plant to Adven, closing early 2027.
  - Completed an ERP upgrade in 2025.
- **Sustainability governance:** a Group Sustainability Director and a corporate-responsibility steering group (p.26).

## Where they stand on climate and nature (Annual Report 2025)

**Climate:**
- **Own operations:** Scope 1+2 was **4,516 t** in 2025, down **80 % since 2019**. The 75 % target was beaten. Renewable energy share: 81 %.
- **Value chain:** total Scope 1–3 was about **218,100 t**. Purchased goods and services are **87.1 %**, mostly raw-material farming. Scope 1+2 is only about 2 %.
- **Farmland emissions not yet counted:** they have *started* specifying an inventory that includes the land-use sector, and *started* exploring how to monitor and reduce farm emissions and carbon sequestration (p.31). **Our angle:** the current farm calculator assumes mineral soil, so peat-soil emissions are missing. That has to be fixed before the SBTi base year is set.
- **Biggest climate-related financial risk:** harvest risk in raw-material procurement. Extreme weather can swing annual harvests and the € impact can be significant (p.32; Financial Review p.7).
- **Material topics** from the double-materiality analysis (approved Apr 2025): climate and energy, biodiversity, own workforce, workers in the value chain. Water is reported as part of biodiversity.

**Nature:**
- **No targets yet:** Apetit says it has *not previously had clearly defined biodiversity targets*. Biodiversity is now a key development area, and water management is a central theme of the new strategy (p.36).
- **Water-protection projects:**
  - A farm-specific water-protection survey and action plan, piloted with the **Pyhäjärvi Institute** in 2025. The Säkylä plant sits next to Lake Pyhäjärvi.
  - The Archipelago Sea water-responsibility project, running since 2024 (p.37).
- **Farm standards:** all Finnish contract growers are FSA-certified; Swedish growers are IP Sigill-certified.

**Reporting:**
- **CDP 2025:** B for climate, B- for water (p.21).
- **No external assurance of sustainability reporting** (GRI index, p.47).
- **"Traceable food chain"** is a strategic sustainability priority, backed by investment in modern information systems (p.25).

## How to use this in the pitch

- **Open with the weather.** "Two harvests in a row, weather cost you money. Your strategy bets on more peas and more Sweden." It isn't their fault, so it lands well with the people who work there.
- **Quantify the CEO's claim.** "Two regions reduce risk. By how much? And what else do you need?" The pack's own docs ask us to quantify this hedge.
- **For the IT judge (Jani Kielenniva):** show that it runs end to end and maps onto Fabric, AI Foundry and Power BI, and how it would plug into their new ERP.
- **For the sustainability judge (Minna Päällysaho):** show correct GHG Protocol and SBTi logic, the peat-soil baseline issue, the approval flow, and the honest limitations.
- **Be tactful about their reporting slips.** The pack's reconciliation doc found transposed digits in the sample workbook. Frame it as "our agent runs these checks automatically on every refresh", never as "you made mistakes".
- **Ask them at the Q&A:** "When did you realise the Swedish crop was failing, and what would you have done with two weeks' warning?" and "Which decision would you use this for: contracted area, irrigation, or the Finland/Sweden split?" Then use their words.
